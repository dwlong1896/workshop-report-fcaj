**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Getting familiar with AWS, Cost Management, and IAM Access Control](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Deploying Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) infrastructure and EC2 Virtual Servers](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [S3 Cloud Storage, RDS Database, and CloudWatch System Monitoring](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Techniques for migrating data and servers to the Cloud](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Docker Application Containerization and Infrastructure Management with CloudFormation](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Administrative Security, Automated Backup, and Cost Optimization](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Microservices Architecture and Serverless Programming with Lambda and DynamoDB](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Cognito User Authentication and SQS & SNS Asynchronous Communication](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9:** [Deploying Serverless Containers with Fargate and Kubernetes EKS Platform](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10:** [Building a Data Lake Platform and Machine Learning with SageMaker](1.10-week10/)
